Security

How connections are protected

Kaishen never asks for your bank or brokerage password, and it does not store one. You sign in on the institution’s own site. Plaid handles that handoff.

What stays on the server

After you connect, Kaishen stores the accounts, balances, transactions, and the token used to refresh that connection. That token stays on the Kaishen server. It is not put in the browser.

Encryption in transit

The app and this website are served over HTTPS. Data moving between Kaishen and Plaid uses HTTPS as well.

What Kaishen does not do

  • It does not see your institution password.
  • It does not move money.
  • It does not sell financial data.
