Budgeting

Banks and brokerages, in one ledger.

Kaishen connects a bank or brokerage to read the accounts, balances, and transactions it needs to show spending and net worth. It does not move money, and it does not sell that data.

You sign in at the institution. Kaishen does not ask for your bank password.

What you can see

  1. Connections

    Each bank or brokerage you link is listed with the accounts that came with it. Add another when you want a second institution on the same ledger.

  2. Spending

    Card transactions show the merchant, the date, and the amount, so a month of spending is one list instead of a stack of statements.

  3. Net worth

    Balances from the accounts you have connected sit side by side, next to the transactions behind them.
