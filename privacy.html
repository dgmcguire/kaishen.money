Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026.

Kaishen is a budgeting app. This page describes what it handles when you connect a bank or brokerage, and what this website does on its own.

What the app collects

When you connect an institution, Kaishen receives:

  • The institution name
  • Account names, types, masks, and balances
  • Transactions, including merchant, date, and amount
  • The token Plaid uses so Kaishen can refresh that connection

Kaishen does not collect your bank or brokerage username or password. You sign in at the institution.

Why it is collected

Kaishen uses that data to show spending, balances, and net worth for the accounts you chose to connect. It does not move money. It does not sell consumer data. It does not share that data with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Connection data is stored on the server that runs Kaishen, so the app can refresh the accounts you linked. Traffic to that server is encrypted with HTTPS. The token that refreshes a connection stays on that server.

Plaid

Account linking is provided by Plaid. Plaid’s own notice for people connecting an account is the Plaid end-user privacy policy.

How long it is kept

Kaishen keeps a connection’s data so it can keep showing those accounts. The data for a connection is deleted when that connection is removed.
